<objective>
Close verification gap G1: make the rotating-body Δv use the body's world centre of mass as the rotation centre, and lock it with a committed regression test that exercises `applyTorque`.

Purpose: `PhysicsWorld.tick()` currently adds the weapon's angular term as `ω×r` with `r` measured from `body.translation()` — the rigid-body origin — while `linvel()` is the velocity of the centre of mass and Rapier rotates about the COM. For the default compound geometry (body 70 kg at the local origin + hammer 25 kg at local x = 0.7) the COM sits 0.1842 m off the origin, so the injected velocity error is `|ω|·0.1842` m/s. The verifier measured the emitted Δv at 18.186 against a COM-correct 9.648 at ω = 46.4 rad/s (88% error), and confirmed Rapier's own `velocityAtPoint()` agrees with the `worldCom()` formula to 2.4e-8. This is a deterministic computation defect in the exact attack class (`Spin` / `Charge`) the WR-02 fix was meant to improve — not a calibration preference. No committed test calls `applyTorque`, which is why it survived review.

Output: a two-line reference-point fix in `src/physics/PhysicsWorld.ts` and a new `tests/physics/rotation.test.ts` that pins the COM-correct Δv and rejects the origin-based value.
</objective>

Inside the `if (hasContactPoint && attackerBody !== undefined && defenderBody !== undefined)` block, replace the two rotation-centre reads so the moment arm origin is each body's world centre of mass: `attackerBody.worldCom()` and `defenderBody.worldCom()`. Keep the existing `r` subtraction, the 2D cross-product form `(-ω·ry, ω·rx)`, and the subsequent normal projection byte-for-byte identical — only the reference point changes. `worldCom()` returns a `Vector` with `x`/`y`, exactly like the call it replaces, so no other line needs to move.

Why: Rapier's `RigidBody.linvel()` returns the velocity of the centre of mass, and the solver rotates the body about its centre of mass. The angular term `ω×r` must therefore be measured from the same point; using the rigid-body origin mixes two different reference points. Do NOT change the velocity sampling instant: `#preVelocities` is filled before `#world.step(...)` and stays the sole Δv source (D-08). The contact point and normal still come from `world.contactPair(...)` exactly as they do today; do not attempt to reconcile the manifold's frame with the pre-step velocities — that is the phase's accepted design, and altering it would change calibrated damage magnitudes beyond this gap.

Do NOT touch `src/physics/types.ts`. `IPhysicsWorld`, `IVelocitySnapshot` (whose `vx`/`vy` are already documented as the centre-of-mass velocity), `IDamageEvent` and every other approved member keep their exact signatures and JSDoc.
