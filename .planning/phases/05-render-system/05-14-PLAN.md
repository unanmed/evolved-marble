<objective>
Fix two UAT-observed combat problems: battles ending almost immediately after two armies meet, and low-health units fleeing forever so fights never conclude.
</objective>

<context>
Investigation (already done): `BattleSystem.recomputeWeightedCenters` uses the raw weighted centroid of current members; with an 80 m trigger and a 40 m membership square only part of each army joins, and dropping boundary units shifts the centre, dropping more units — a positive-feedback collapse. `endBattles` then sees one team and `everJoinedTeams` has both sides, so it finishes immediately. Reproduced: default 8v8 ends at tick 46, 4v4 at tick 31, both with living units on both sides.
The new disengage AI is NOT involved (0 calls in those runs).
</context>
