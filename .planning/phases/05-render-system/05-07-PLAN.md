<objective>
Implement the two Pixi adapters that turn the pure models into a frame: `Hud` (bars, level numbers, pooled damage numbers/sparks/death rings, and the screen-space time+speed info bar) and `Renderer` (the layer graph, static map, camera application, unit-view reconciliation with culling and flash, strategic markers, HUD frame assembly, and exactly one `app.render()` per call), plus the module-level `createRenderer` factory.

Purpose: this is the first plan that can actually paint a frame. It must stay a thin adapter: every decision comes from `cameraMath`, `frameModel`, `hudModel` or the config, and the only new behaviour is *when* to read, create, destroy and paint. The two rules that matter most are "exactly one world tick and one render per frame" and "the renderer owns no loop" — they are what let Phase 6 grab a canvas per frame without a hidden ticker racing it.

Output: `src/render/Hud.ts`, `src/render/Renderer.ts` (with `createRenderer`), and an extended import-boundary guard.
</objective>
