Contract shape carried from the locked decisions (all present at the gate for confirmation):
- **D-01:** REND-05 is relaxed — no fps contract, no delta-time, no LOD; only basic optimisation shapes (`ICullQuery`, pool caps). Every performance statement stays Round-2 calibration.
- **D-02:** sequential battle playback / returning to 15 s belongs to Phase 6. The camera has a single explicit target and no queue, timeline or rewind type.
- **D-03:** the visual seam is `IUnitView` + `IUnitViewFactory`; the default implementation is geometry, a sprite implementation may replace it later, and `IRenderer`/`ICamera`/`IHud` never change.
- **D-04:** faction identity is a configurable colour on `IUnitVisualSpec.factionColor`; class identity is data only (`classKind`, kept for legend/debug); the 6 weapons and 2 armours are keyed by the frozen enums and drawn on the physical footprint supplied by `IRenderConfig.geometry`.
- **D-05:** `IRenderPalette` encodes the dark-base / high-saturation tone (background, map floor, grid, terrain, text, health bar, weapon, armour, flash, neutral).
- **D-06:** `IEffectsConfig` declares the strong-feedback budget — flash frames, particles per hit, particle/damage-number/death-effect lifetimes and hard caps, and the shake threshold.
- **D-07:** `IHudConfig.healthBarSeconds = 15` and `alwaysVisibleBelowUnits = 6`; `IHudUnitState.healthVisible` and `IHudFrame.frameIndex` make the "15 × outputFps frames after a hit, always on below 6 living units" rule expressible without any wall-clock. Bars carry no text and no unit name.
- **D-08:** `IHudUnitState.level` + `IHudConfig.levelWorldHeight`; the level is a HUD-layer element drawn upright (never inside the rotating child) and always world-scaled, never screen-constant.
- **D-09:** `IHudConfig.screenBarWidthPx`, `screenScaleAt`, `worldScaleAt` and `IHudUnitState.hudScale` express the hybrid mode — screen-constant up close, world-sized far out, smooth in between; `screenScaleAt` must stay above `worldScaleAt`.
- **D-10:** `IEffectsConfig.damageNumberThreshold = 50`; only large hits get a rising, fading number, light hits only flash/spark.
- **D-11:** the video info bar reads `IHudFrame.gameMinutes` + `minutesPerTick` ("第 x 年 x 月 x 日 HH:MM / N×"); no faction legend and no combat statistics exist in the HUD model. Calendar formatting is a Round-2 pure function.
- **D-12:** the operator panel is an HTML/CSS overlay outside the canvas; the render contract exposes only the seams it drives (`getCamera`, `setBattleTarget`, `reset`, `getCanvas`, `IRenderServices.world`, `frameDelayMs`) and declares no DOM type.
- **D-13:** `IRenderConfig.width`/`height` with the 1920×1080 defaults materialised in Round-2 `constants.ts`; the backing store is fixed and the page scales it.
- **D-14:** `CameraMode.Strategic` = whole map + army arrows (heading, strength) + city markers (kind), no unit views; `CameraMode.BattleFocus` = fit the targeted battle. `IStrategicArmyMarker`/`IStrategicCityMarker`/`IStrategicFrame` carry no unit ids, so the "no units in strategic view" rule is structural.
- **D-15:** the follow target is supplied only through `ICamera.setBattleTarget(battleId | null)`; Phase 5 never scores or selects battles.
- **D-16:** the minimal demo page and its `dev` script are Round 2; this round adds no entry point.
- **D-17:** the renderer owns no tick; `frameDelayMs` is documented as a runner-only delay and `outputFps` as HUD-frame-count/encoding metadata.
- **D-18:** `IRenderer.renderFrame()` paints exactly one frame and `getCanvas()` exposes the backing store; pacing, pause, step and reset are the caller's job.
- **D-19:** `IRenderServices` + `RenderServicesProvider` let the real composition root (physics → sensing → damage sink → control → kingdom world) hand rendering its read-only dependencies without the renderer knowing how they were built; `IWorld` still exposes no control/physics.
- **D-20:** `IRenderConfig` with `palette`/`camera`/`hud`/`effects` sub-configs is the single tuning entry; no magic numbers elsewhere.
- **D-21:** `ICameraConfig.coverage` (0.9) is documented as a property of the TARGET composition; the current transform may lag during a smooth switch, and no transition may teleport.
- **D-22:** the `?classes=all` demo variant is Round 2 demo data and needs no contract change — `IUnitVisualSpec` already carries all 6 `WeaponKind`s and both `ArmorKind`s.
- **D-23:** the level sits at the body centre (~0.7 m, white with a dark outline), never rotates and always follows camera zoom — expressed as `IHudUnitState.level` + `IHudConfig.levelWorldHeight` plus the JSDoc placement rule; the exact pixel/world placement is Round 2.
