Contract shape carried from the locked decisions (all present at the gate for confirmation):
- **D-01:** one global `IPhysicsWorld` holds every unit for life; `IArmy` is a macro container (center/heading/roster/state), never a physics or battle entity.
- **D-02:** battles are unit-level — `IBattle.getMemberUnitIds()` is a unit-id set; no army concept exists at the battle layer.
- **D-03:** battles run concurrently; `IBattleSystem.listActiveBattles/listCompletedBattles/drainEvents` provide the ordered playback queue for Phase 6/7.
- **D-04:** overlapping battle areas merge (earlier `battleId` wins; the absorbed one ends with `mergedIntoBattleId`).
- **D-05:** units keep their original `armyId` after a battle; re-trigger requires >80 m.
- **D-06:** units persist across battles (health/XP/level/equipment); death is permanent.
- **D-07/D-08:** no wall-clock tickrate — 1× (battle) is 1 tick = 1 game minute, 24× is 1 tick = 24 game minutes; every strategic timer counts game minutes.
- **D-09:** any active battle forces the global rate to 1×.
- **D-10:** substep mapping is an implementation choice (research: one physics substep = one game minute; 24× = 24 substeps; `DT` never changes).
- **D-11/D-14:** march/battle/brake switching rides the frozen `IUnitAI` seam via a delegating AI reading `IUnitModeSource` — no change to `IUnitAI`/`IUnitAIProvider`/`IControlSystem`.
- **D-12/D-13:** army center 10 m/h with edge bounce; formation inside a 25 m circle; march heal 1/h, garrison heal 2/h (both need G-1).
- **D-15/D-19:** same-team units collide but take no damage; only same-battle + both-inside-area pairs settle — all filtering lives in the round-2 `IDamageSink`.
- **D-16/D-17/D-18:** trigger at <80 m between army centers (or army center ↔ city point); battle area is an 80 m axis-aligned square (half-extent 40) centred on the `UnitWeight`-weighted centroid (default = level, injected).
- **D-20/D-21/D-22:** battle ends when only one faction remains inside; city battles then hand the city to the winner, may garrison via `GarrisonSelector`, and send the loser's survivors back toward their own city.
- **D-23:** training 96 h (normal) / 72 h (capital) per unit, garrison caps by city size, training class configurable, attributes via `spawnUnit`.
- **D-24/D-25:** all kingdom types live in `src/kingdom/types.ts`; vision is a stateless query (army 100 m ∪ city 150 m), never a remembered map.
- **D-26/D-27/D-28/D-29:** cities are points with no collider; blocking terrain lives near cities and blocks movement + rays; the world is built from a configurable `IScenario` with a small default; a minimal replaceable `ICommander` (Phase 7 swaps it wholesale) issues `IArmyOrder`s.

Naming/placement refinements versus 04-RESEARCH §Recommended Contract Shape (all visible at the gate): `IArmy.getGarrisonedCityId()` (instead of `getCityId`, to pair unambiguously with `ICity.getGarrisonArmyId()`); `ITimeSystem` gains `advanceTick()` so `getTickIndex()` has a writer (C-17); the internal `IMutableFaction/IArmy/ICity/IBattle` write interfaces are declared now at the file end (C-7) so round-2 classes never grow public members outside an interface (AGENTS §5); `IVec2` stays in the kingdom folder (A13/C-4); and — closing research flag A21 — the vision queries return the new reduced `IVisibleArmyBrief`/`IVisibleCityBrief` instead of full `IArmy`/`ICity`, so enemy roster/order/training internals are unreachable through vision while `IArmy`/`ICity` remain the god-view and World-internal reads.

Open gate decisions: **G-1** (`IHealableUnit` + `Unit.heal`), **G-2** (`IStaticObstacleWorld` + `PhysicsWorld.addStaticObstacle/removeStaticObstacle`), **C-2** (`spawnUnit` return type — round-2 choice, never a silent cast), **G-3** initial battle membership, **G-4** calibratable defaults, the **A21 visibility granularity** (reduced briefs), the city-loser target, projectile deferral, city capture without `updateTeam`, the `IWorld` read surface, map-boundary walls, `ITimeSystem.advance` visibility, and the two round-2 seams **C-12** (BattleSystem reads time through an injected closure) / **C-15** (training output registers through `ControlSystem.registerUnit`). Rejecting G-1 or G-2 must be recorded as an explicit downgrade, never a silent drop.
