Turn the marching/battle core into a living kingdom: armies can garrison a city, cities train new units on game time under a hard garrison cap, marching and garrisoned units heal at 1/h and 2/h through the G-1 seam, vision answers the 100 m army / 150 m city union with reduced briefs and no memory, and blocking terrain plus four map-boundary walls become real Rapier geometry through the G-2 seam.

Output: city training/garrison behaviour, healing wiring, three visibility queries, terrain + boundary-wall physics, and four extended/new test suites (`march` additions, `city`, `vision`, `terrain`).
